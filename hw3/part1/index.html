Hello! My name is Carson Hunter and I'm a third year Data Science and Biology student at Northeastern University. I love running, working out at the gym, trying new restaurants, and learning about new topics. A topic I've always loved to research is urbanism. Urbansim encompasses many topics, including walkability, good street design, and public transit. By implementing principles of good urbanism, cities can become safer, healthier, and more enjoyable to live in. North American cities, after being built around cars, have abandoned many of these principles, while the majority of the developed world has embraced said concepts. With the exception of few US cities, most American downtowns are interwoven with 4 lane highways and parking lots. Given the large amount of land these spaces, which are designed for cars, occupy, walkers find themselves separated from neighborhoods, crossing high speed roads, and far away from daily activities. This has made the ownership of a car almost required in the majority of American urban areas. Recently, there has been an increasing push for more thoughtfully designed downtowns, where one is in close proximity, or can easily access, most of their daily essentials by either walking, cycling, or utilizing public transit. This concept, which incorporates density, good transit, and ample pedestrian space, is referred to as the 15 Minute City.
