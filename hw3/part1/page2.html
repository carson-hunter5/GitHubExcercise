The 15 Minute City

The 15 Minute City refers to a new philosophy in urban design advocating for cities to be built in a manner that allows residents to access most daily essentials in 15 minutes, without a car. This incentivizes more trips being taken with sustainable modes of transportation, including walking, biking, or using public transportation. This introduces benefits like:

  • Healthier Cities- residents using more active modes of transit means more time spent getting excercise rather than sitting in cars. This is shown to have drastic benefits on mental and phsyical health. It would also reduce pollution, reducing respiratory problems.
  • Better Environment - Cars contribute significantly to greenhouse gas emmisions every year. More utilization of sustainable modes of transit would cut these emmisions immensely.
  • Quality of Life - Walkable cities allow residents to not have to worry about the hassle of maintaining, fueling, and parking a personal vehicle every day. People also get to engage with their cities at a personal level rather than through metal boxes.
